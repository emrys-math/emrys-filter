# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Goal

EmrysFilter exists to make state estimation algorithms available and useful to all who want or need them.  Its contributor community welcomes anyone who wishes to advance this goal.  We are united in this respect; we need not agree in any other respects.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: the repository's issues, pull requests, GitHub Discussions, and commit history, and any other venue the project runs.  It also applies to private communication between contributors that arises from the project, and to conduct elsewhere that is directed at a contributor because of their part in the project.

In this Code of Conduct, "discussion" refers to any communication within this scope, not only GitHub Discussions.

## Topics of Discussion

Constructive discussion and debate are encouraged on topics directly relevant to the project.  Examples of relevant topics include:

* The choice of algorithms to implement and how they should be cited
* The correctness and security of the algorithms' implementations
* The clarity, consistency, and robustness of the design
* The balance between simplicity, flexibility, and performance
* The project's organization and the process by which its development is managed

Discussion of topics tangentially related to the project should be kept short and should be redirected to a more relevant venue as soon as practical.

Discussion of topics unrelated to the project is not appropriate in project spaces.  This applies even when friendly and welcomed by the participants, because other community members may disagree, sometimes strongly, on any topic beyond our unifying goal.  Careless digression is a needless threat to our cohesion.

## Rules of Discussion

* Arguments must be based on facts as much as possible.
* Arguments must not be based on anyone's personal characteristics, positive or negative.
* Statements of preference, opinion, and judgment are welcome if they are acknowledged as such.  Explanations of why one holds these opinions are encouraged.
* Relevant anecdotes from personal experience are welcome in support of judgment calls, but are not decisive; the future may not be like the past.
* All contributors must make a good-faith effort to moderate the emotional temperature of discussions.  We all care deeply about the subject, and this will naturally tend to escalate tension.  We must all exercise conscientious self-control and show grace and understanding toward others.
* All discussion must be expressed in language appropriate to a professional workplace.  Formality is not required, but basic politeness is.
* Any contributor may ask another not to contact them privately, and that request must be honored.
* No threats to any person's safety, security, or privacy, explicit or implied, will be tolerated.

## The Maintainer's Role

The maintainer is the final arbiter of the content of the EmrysFilter library, its development process, and its Code of Conduct.

The maintainer holds authority because someone must.  The maintainer pledges to exercise humility in their role, acknowledging that many others have knowledge and judgment that equal or exceed their own.

## Reporting

Violations of this Code of Conduct should be reported to the maintainer at myrddinemrys0440@gmail.com.  All complaints will be reviewed and investigated fairly.  The maintainer will respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

In the event that the maintainer violates this Code of Conduct, a report may be submitted to GitHub through [GitHub's abuse reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).  This may be done in addition to or instead of notifying the maintainer, at the reporter's discretion.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will follow these guidelines in determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Behaviors**:

* A minor violation
* An inadvertent violation, especially one likely to have resulted from a lack of understanding of the maintainer's subjective interpretation of the standards
* A violation resulting from momentarily elevated emotion

**Consequence**: A private, written correction providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate.

### 2. Warning

**Behaviors**:

* A violation that is both significant and deliberate
* A serious violation, regardless of intent

**Consequence**: A private, written warning stating clearly the nature of the violation and the consequences for continued similar behavior.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Behaviors**:

* An extremely serious violation
* A pattern of serious or deliberate violations
* Retaliation against someone who has reported a violation or against the maintainer for enforcement actions
* A threat to anyone's safety, security, or privacy
* An actual violation of anyone's privacy

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time.  No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with the maintainer, is allowed during this period.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Behaviors**:

* Violation of the terms of a temporary ban
* Continued violations after having served a temporary ban
* A serious and credible threat to anyone's safety or security
* An actual violation of anyone's safety or security

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any communication channel the maintainer controls.
